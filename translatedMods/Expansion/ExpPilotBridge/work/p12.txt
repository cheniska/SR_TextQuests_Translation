556	The gaalians were the first to encounter the klissans, many hundreds of years before their contact with the other races of the Coalition. At that time gaalian civilization did not consider insatiability a mark of barbarism, nor self-knowledge and moderation marks of wisdom, and pursued active galactic expansion. Having settled many worlds in neighboring sectors, the gaalians wanted to reach the neighboring spiral arm of the Galaxy. This was considered nearly impossible, since entering and leaving hyperspace is only possible where the continuum is curved by the gravity of especially massive objects such as stars, and for "piercing" the continuum between two zones of curvature a ship spends more energy the greater the distance between them. But between the spiral arms stretch countless parsecs of emptiness; piercing them in a single go would require energy on the order of a star's. Of course, one could fly along the spiral, first along one's own arm to the center of the Galaxy and then back along the second arm - but such a route is too long even for the gaalians. However, the gaalians managed to discover a technology for creating stable "bubbles" of curved space that can move independently of the mass that produced them. In such a "bubble" one can travel to virtually any point in space.
557	Thus the gaalians reached the neighboring arm and founded their first colonies in the system of a star they named Eltan. From there the expansion continued successfully, until one of the flotillas exploring the second arm happened to meet a Makhpella. The Makhpella was a gigantic intelligent ship, a product of non-biological evolution. The Makhpella moved from star to star, surrounded by a fleet of smaller "ships" - klissans - subordinate to it. The very first attempt at contact ended tragically - the klissans destroyed the gaalian expedition. So began a war in which the gaalians suffered one defeat after another. Not only the Fleet's crews perished - upon reaching colonized planets, the Makhpella and the klissans destroyed all living things on them.
558	The gaalians who remained in our arm escaped the danger, but the shock they had endured influenced the entire subsequent history of their civilization. The idea of space expansion abruptly lost popularity. The "bubble" technologies were banned and classified. The intellectual loss was great, too - many scientists had hurried in their time to the second arm, the cutting edge of the unknown, and were now dead.
559	Over the centuries since then, the gaalians recovered somewhat, began exploring space again, established contact with the faeyans, pelengs, maloqs and humans, and helped to create the Galaxy Council. And then, three hundred years ago, when it seemed that a lasting peace had finally been achieved, Rachekhan spoiled everything. He was one of the best commanders of the peleng fleet, but he was passed over for promotion and decided to become a pirate. First, though, he worked in intelligence and managed to obtain from the gaalians an archive that they themselves no longer remembered. From it he learned both the secret of the "bubbles" and about the Makhpella. He guessed, and rightly as it turned out, that there are in fact many Makhpellas. At first, though, he had no need of the Makhpella - the "bubble" was quite enough to pirate with impunity throughout all explored space, appearing suddenly anywhere and vanishing again into hyperspace.

The first, of course, did not work out for him, but the second did - the Makhpella and the klissans, chasing him, ended up in the other arm.

So both the first and the second Makhpella, upon meeting spaceships, took them for their intelligent kin and their crews for organic parasites. 564	<clr>Internal conflicts in faeyan history:<clrEnd> It is interesting and noteworthy that the faeyan civilization knew no concept of weapons for a very long time. The strong humanitarian content of faeyan culture is the reason for the complete absence of traditional wars fought with weapons of physical elimination. Although at various stages of development there were varying numbers of cities and autonomous settlements, conflicts between them never grew into clashes aimed at mutual destruction.<br>The only case of the death of a dalani and his entourage occurred when emissaries of an opposing city accused him of stupidity (the greatest vice for the faeyans), and he, staking his life, lost a contest of wits (eylaki) and put himself to death, although his subjects begged him not to. As a result, his opponent was recognized as superior to him in aiona (reason) and accepted as the new dalani. This single case of death resulting from political confrontation occurred at an early stage of development, when no organized forms of "arbitration of reason" yet existed. In all other cases of conflict, everything was settled by intellectual disputes (aiollita), in which opponents spent time in discussion and reflection. Then both acknowledged that one of them was superior and wiser than the other, and so the conflict was resolved.
