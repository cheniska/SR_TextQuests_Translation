610	<clr>The start of the policy of racial peace:<clrEnd> In time, human diplomacy and principles of law came to play a leading role in the Galaxy. The entire prehistory of the emergence of the Galactic Coalition is the story of human diplomacy's consistent work to ease tensions. As excellent psychologists and diplomats, humans began to pursue a policy of "racial peace". This policy, formulated back in the period of the early faeyan-human alliance and having proved itself splendidly, consisted of establishing clear rights and obligations for each side, developing economic and cultural ties, and holding a constant dialogue on a regular basis. Such were the relations between humans and faeyans. When the confrontation with the pelengs gained strength and the aggressive Peleng-Maloq bloc arose, human diplomacy began seeking ways to dissolve the two opposing alliances and form on their basis a single union in which all would have equal rights and whose benefits would be obvious to all participants.
611	<clr>Formation of the Galactic Coalition:<clrEnd> All the events described above finally cleared the way for human efforts to build a clear system of galactic law. These attempts first took shape with the introduction, in 2705, of the Galactic Congress - a deliberative body of all five races that discussed economic and cultural cooperation. Because some conflicts were still going on, a galactic confederation called the Galactic Coalition was formed in 2750. The Coalition Charter was based on the principles of human law, and human diplomatic institutions formed the basis of the union's bodies. A single credit system and a single chronological scale were introduced (the latter again became the human calendar, "from the birth of Christ").
