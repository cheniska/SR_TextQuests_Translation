589	<clr>The formation of states and the age of dynasties:<clrEnd> With the emergence of state formations, writing, and the first material discoveries, Maloq civilization took the path of strengthening the authority of the rart, enlarging the rartan, and devising new forms of suppressing internal and external enemies.<br>The political form remained, and remains, unchanged - the unlimited or nearly unlimited power of the ruler.
590	<clr>Interracial relations:<clrEnd> For objective reasons, the space age began fairly late - after many millennia of slow and thorny development. The main reason that forced the maloqs to search diligently for new worlds was the extreme overpopulation of Ramgatru.<br>Thanks to their ability to adapt to practically any conditions, the maloqs began rapidly expanding their colonial borders, despite not knowing bioassimilation or landscape transformation technologies. Even without them, however, the maloq race took root splendidly on the harshest and frankly hostile planets.
591	<clr>Political and cultural environment:<clrEnd> At the present stage of development, Maloq civilization is a vast territory consisting of independent rartan planets united in the advisory body of the Argot. A huge armed fleet continues to be built up and maintained, and intensive colonization is under way.
592	<clr>Specifics of the maloq economy:<clrEnd> For traditional reasons, the maloqs use an extremely small number of minerals in construction and other fields. At the same time, however, they mine them in large quantities for export, since minerals are in great demand among the other races of the Coalition.<br>Because there is no developed public medicine, the maloqs are in acute need of imported medicines. Weapons and equipment are traditionally in high demand.

594	<clr>Peleng<clrEnd> are a race of amphibians who inhabit the lower regions of the Gurg Belt. The pelengs are notorious for their destructive politics, espionage, and piracy. Not being an intellectually oriented race, Peleng develops poorly in technology but constantly replenishes its scientific resources through espionage.<br>Peleng has neither diplomatic nor trade ties and takes only a formal part in the work of Coalition bodies.
595	<clr>Biological features of the species:<clrEnd> The peleng race belongs to the species of gill-necked amphibians, commonly called lepraz. This species is equally well adapted to living in a liquid medium and on land, and can breathe any gas mixtures that include a small amount of nitrogen. Like all lepraz, the pelengs eat acid plankton and kalol reef deposits.
596	<clr>Origin of civilization:<clrEnd> The oceanic civilization of the pelengs originates in the liquid acid environment of the small planet Rakhish at the center of the Gurg Belt. Rakhish's fauna is a unique system whose main feature is phenomenal lepraz animal forms with varying levels of intellectual development.
597	<clr>The lepraz phenomenon and its role in the formation of peleng civilization:<clrEnd> Lepraz forms have long been studied as a biological and social phenomenon. In essence, the lepraz are amphibians united by an almost identical body structure but by completely different levels of mental development. They all have the same physiology and look so alike that on casual observation they cannot be told apart. Yet they are completely different species, as is evident at least from the fact that crossbreeding of the species produces sterile hybrids.<br>In terms of intellect, lepraz forms also range from primitive animals to fully intelligent beings. Thus the glamigids, the least developed of the lepraz, are capable neither of tool use, nor of communication, nor of any form of social community. The fumia-lyakus already have the rudiments of articulation and can communicate by gestures. The fumia-prodonts have developed articulation, use tools, and build reef buildings. The dradans and riptungs, almost intelligent forms, have a developed language, live in group settlements, and develop materially and culturally. The pelengs are the only fully intelligent lepraz form, inhabiting Rakhish. The entire history of the formation and development of Peleng civilization is shaped by their interaction with their less intelligent kin.
598	<clr>Specifics of peleng statehood:<clrEnd> In time the pelengs developed forms of government that were at first classical despotisms. Discipline was harsh, as was necessary for waging numerous wars. The wars, however, were easy, since at first the pelengs faced fairly undeveloped peoples, mostly the lyus and zangs. The results of such wars were cultural rather than economic - they honed in the peleng mentality a sense of insurmountable superiority over other intelligent beings. The pelengs developed a persistent disgust for their lesser kin, who became for them merely objects of harassment and plunder.<br>In the end, all this led to plunder and banditry against the junior lepraz being regarded as a natural and even honorable occupation. In some regions cannibalism gained strength, with pelengs eating the lower lepraz (mostly glamigids and lyus). And since the physiological boundary between pelengs and other species is quite arbitrary, even when a peleng was murdered it was easy to prove that the victim was not a peleng at all. As a result the very concept of crime became quite shaky in peleng culture. The right of might began to prevail over the rule of law, the principle of profit and convenience over the principle of humanity.
599	<clr>Interracial relations:<clrEnd> The pelengs' attitude toward other intelligent beings they met in space was at first the same as toward the Rakhish shrakans. The image of an equal intelligent being, a partner-being, simply did not fit in the peleng mind.<br>On meeting a faeyan diplomatic convoy, Peleng attacked it out of habit and was defeated. The faeyans' requests for negotiations achieved nothing, and the war that flared up continued until the faeyans' superior forces left the pelengs with no way out. An ultimatum was presented, and for the first time Peleng acknowledged an unconditional surrender.<br>Thus began a new stage of confrontation - black diplomacy, espionage, and legalized piracy. The notorious peleng lyakushas took on a new quality and cosmic scale.
600	<clr>Administrative and economic conditions:<clrEnd> The peleng planets coordinate their actions in a rather weak body, the Shazikhmin (usually translated as "the great gorge"). The powers granted to the Shazikhmin are small, which explains the inconsistency and instability of Peleng's policy as a space civilization. The main role is played by the policies of individual planets and their leaders. Internal wars are also frequent, when planets form hostile camps and armed conflicts break out between them. In such cases the Galaxy Council prefers to stand aside.
601	<clr>Foreign policy:<clrEnd> Peleng's attempts to overcome its technological lag consist of a developed system of industrial espionage. The peleng directorate Dzuhallag is the best - if the word is permissible here at all - espionage and sabotage structure in the entire Coalition. Dzuhallag's industrial and information special operations cause no small trouble for the governments of the faeyans, as well as of humans and gaalians.<br>Thanks to technological espionage, the pelengs manage to maintain a relatively decent technological level; moreover, they actively resell stolen information resources to Maloq.

603	<clr>Humanity<clrEnd> is a race of humanoids that inhabit the regions from the Wasteland to the Montel Confederate Territory. Humanity conducts active diplomatic work in all bodies of the Coalition and is responsible for coordinating the work of its bodies, in particular the Galaxy Council, as well as for peace initiatives.<br>This is a race oriented first and foremost toward humanitarian and cultural values. Humans are also well developed politically.
604	<clr>Export of human culture:<clrEnd> Humanity gave modern galactic civilization the concept of culture as an unproductive form of activity with playful content, which is generally unfamiliar to the other intelligent races. Humans introduced and popularized such phenomena as tourism, theater and video art, romantic fiction, and virtual games. Unfortunately, human culture also brought negative things, such as alcoholic drinks and gambling, which became popular on all the planets of the Coalition. Humans' traditionally superb alcoholic drinks are still in high demand on human planets. A fight is under way against the huge flows of drug smuggling.
605	<clr>Biological features of the species:<clrEnd> Humans belong to the class of oxygen-breathing humanoids and are characterized as protohumanoids. They are of average height, equally developed physically and mentally, and eat organic protein food. The diversity of climatic regimes on most inhabited planets (especially in the Solar system) is reflected in strong national differences, expressed above all in skin color and facial features.
606	<clr>Origin of civilization:<clrEnd> Original human culture originates on the planet Earth, which is covered mostly by water. The scattered land areas and the planet's highly rugged terrain caused quite noticeable fragmentation among human communities, especially in the initial stage of development.
607	<clr>The development of monarchism and globalization:<clrEnd> With time, tribal forms began to die out and be replaced by larger national entities. Whereas in primitive tribal communities the ruler was the oldest member of the tribe, in the larger national communities clearer and more formal forms of power appeared. Countries (states) arose - groups whose members were united by some commonality of national traits. Politically, such countries were at first despotisms, and later monarchies began to take shape.
