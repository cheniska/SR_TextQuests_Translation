566	<clr>Galactic politics:<clrEnd> Politically, the Faey civilization is united in the so-called Onakan Register, a confederative political body. The ambassador to the Galaxy Council is chosen from among outstanding and most authoritative scholar-dalani. The faeyans' main role and direct responsibility in the Coalition is research projects and fields. It was faeyan institutes that conducted and completed, in 2882, the fundamental research into the so-called "black holes" (in fact, wormholes), which led to the creation of null-transition technology and the combat prototypes of the modern vortex projector.<br>The general cultural closeness of faeyans and humans has ensured a certain integration of these races. Although some cultural features point to a strong, at times irreconcilable difference between the two civilizations, integration processes still prevail, and humans and faeyans cooperate constructively in many areas.<br>Toward their former enemies, the maloqs and pelengs, the faeyans' political line is traditionally reserved, if not negative. Both of these races run intensive espionage campaigns aimed at acquiring faeyan technology. The faeyan administration is wary of the expansionist policy of Maloq, which has already seized part of the originally faeyan Aylay sector. Moreover, both maloqs and pelengs feel a hidden (and often open) dislike for the faeyans because of their hermaphroditism.
567	<clr>Economic features:<clrEnd> There is a serious economic problem with food. The extreme rarity, even uniqueness, of the neon organic food that the faeyan race requires and that grows only on Eipentak has given rise to extremely intensive research in food technology. Because of the extreme difficulty of planting neon organics in the soil of alien planets, it is in high demand. Faeyan industry is also pursuing another direction - neon adaptation of flora, which consists of genetically modifying the food of other races to obtain neon forms in subsequent generations. All this requires enormous investments of resources and information. The problem of guaranteeing the faeyan race a food supply is still unsolved, which creates obvious obstacles to colonizing new worlds.<br>Faeyan governments ban trade in drugs, alcohol, and weapons, which are traditionally unpleasant to the faeyans.
568	<clr>Biological features of the species:<clrEnd> The faeyans belong to the humanoid class, but because of their hermaphroditism and their non-standard brain and respiratory structure they are also called semi-humanoids. They breathe a neon-containing air mixture, which explains the violet tint that predominates in their bodies; their food is the traditional neon organics of Eipentak. The faeyans have divided compound-eye vision; they are sedentary and not very physically strong, since most of their body's energy is spent on brain activity.<br>Unique is the paradox of faeyan brain and eye structure, called the phenomenon of shared consciousness: a faeyan can voluntarily divide their consciousness and compound vision into sections that function completely independently, thus forming any number of autonomously and independently acting areas of consciousness, each responsible for its own function and separately perceiving the world through its own part of the compound vision.
569	<clr>Origin of civilization:<clrEnd> The cradle of the faeyan race is the planet Eipentak at the center of the Aylay branch. With an atmosphere of noble gases and unique neon flora and fauna, this planet gave rise to a distinctive faeyan culture centered on the mind in all its manifestations. The constant search for and analysis of information, the refinement of thinking techniques, and the diverse schools and traditions of consciousness have made this culture, on the one hand, dynamic and mobile, and on the other, extremely resistant to attempts to reform it from outside. Faeyan society can rebuild itself easily and quickly, but is hardly open to attempts at change from other cultures.<br>Ani aiona is a cultural phenomenon and a philosophy of existence. Faeyan civilization had no traditional period of archaism or nomadic forms. From the very start of their history the faeyans grouped around the most enlightened leaders - the dalani. They took on functions reminiscent of the traditional functions of rulers, yet different from them. This is how ethnic groups and the first states were formed.<br>The first dalani were the founders of the first classical ani aiona ("life of the mind") - schools, or traditions, of thinking technique (in common galactic translated terminology, "aionic systems"). The first such systems were merely sets of basic principles of one approach or another to the mind and its functioning. Later they increasingly took on a finished scientific shape, methodologies were formed, and the ranks of followers and researchers grew. The history of the ani aiona in the totality of their manifestations, in their coexistence and mutual influence, is the history of faeyan culture. It was the aionic schools, clothed in forms of communal living among students, that became first settlements and later, in more developed forms, cities and states. Thus the mind became the most important priority and object of perfection, and information and the means of obtaining it became the main category of all relations between individuals and groups.
570	<clr>Intangible culture:<clrEnd> When speaking of faeyan intangible culture, what is meant first of all is not a system of unproductive output with an element of play, but, again, the results and goals of mental perfection. The faeyans created magnificent monuments of aionic literature and painting, which were in essence the methodology of one aionic system or another and were created by the dalani and their students. Such literature and painting, according to human art historians, also have artistic value. Today aionic video art, theater, and virtual forms of art exist and flourish, although they are not traditional for the faeyans and arose from a synthesis of faeyan and human cultures.<br>Unlike humans, however, the faeyans are alien to the acute inner perception of beauty as a supra-psychic phenomenon. Their mind, their consciousness, is much more pragmatic and at times even cynical than the human mind. They are more rational and therefore less inclined to perceive beauty, which is irrational by nature. The problems of unproductive culture within the system of aionic philosophies are today studied by the joint faeyan-human Institute for the Development of Mind on Umartanish, and joint cultural projects are handled by the concerns "Ela Aiona Cultra" and "Euro-Kinematon".<br>Faeyan culture, best expressed on the historic homeland of Eipentak, traditionally attracts the attention of researchers and tourists. The biocities of Elmanti and Ansa, the Institute for the Development of Mind on Umartanish, and the flying kuncan farms in the Aiproti valley have become tourist attractions of galactic scale. On Eipentak all elements of traditional faeyan culture are carefully preserved. At the same time, on the more remote faeyan planets an international, cosmopolitan way of life prevails to a greater extent.
571	<clr>Statehood and humanitarian values:<clrEnd> Traditional faeyan historiography divides the history of its civilization into three stages, or paths: the free path, the path of authorities, and onnaraika (the path of new horizons). These are very distinctive, purely faeyan forms of political organization that have survived almost unchanged to this day. They did not always follow one another in chronological order and often overlapped in time. The reason for the changes of these forms was the political will of the leading dalani. All three forms are characterized by their own arsenals of information and political means and their own systems of ani aiona, and therefore require separate consideration.
572	<clr>The free path:<clrEnd> The initial phase of development, the faeyan analog of anarchism. In the usual sense, anarchism as a lawless form of cohabitation is generally not characteristic of faeyan culture, because disorder as such is alien to them. For the first millennia faeyan settlements existed autonomously, each developing its own aionic system and its own dialect. Economic forms of interaction also existed. The faeyans moved freely and founded new cities freely. There was no centralization of power. In common galactic terminology the "free path" is typical anarchism, yet it is at the same time a distinctive faeyan variant of anarchism. With some changes it exists today on many faeyan planets.
573	<clr>The path of authorities:<clrEnd> This path is the faeyan version of a republican system and differs from the preceding form only in the centralization of power in the hands of prominent dalani and in a new scale of economic relations. When the path of authorities in its traditional form became established in most faeyan cities, a sharp demographic and economic growth took place, and the aionic systems acquired the features of independent scientific disciplines. It was in this period that dynamically accelerating technology made a breakthrough into space possible.
574	<clr>Onnaraika (the path of new horizons):<clrEnd> In aionic systems this path appears as a unique form of coexistence of the universal and the earthly, the public and the private, while from a general galactic standpoint it is a confederative ideocratic democracy with divided rights of access to information. This form of civilization arose with the onset of interplanetary fragmentation, and its essence is hard to grasp for those with only a superficial acquaintance with faeyan culture. In the most general terms it is a democracy with state-managed information flows, where each citizen possesses only the information needed to be of the greatest benefit to society.
575	<clr>Traditional infrastructure:<clrEnd> Economically, the faeyan states were fairly homogeneous, which is explained by the relative uniformity of Eipentak's natural resources. Soft and poor rock deposits were traditionally mined, and applied products were made from neon flora. Eipentak is relatively poor in minerals, and the neon plant organisms that serve the faeyans as food grow so slowly that this required the development of specialized branches of farming industry. Cities exchanged food and minerals, the most liquid commodities after information.

577	<clr>Gaal<clrEnd> are a race of tailed humanoid aproids who inhabit the area of the Ona-o Eta branch and have colonies in the center of the Galaxy, which is inaccessible to hyperjumps. The gaalians are the oldest, most powerful, and, to the other civilizations of the Coalition, least known civilization. Its roots go back to the early history of the formation of the Galaxy's star systems and are lost in the chronicles of the uglans, the first humanoids.
578	<clr>Biological features of the species:<clrEnd> The gaalians are the last of the humanoids to retain tails, otherwise known as aproids. They are covered with light, pale hair, breathe nitrogen mixtures, and eat organic substances obtained by molecular synthesis. The gaalians are direct descendants of the legendary uglans, who mysteriously disappeared millions of years ago.
