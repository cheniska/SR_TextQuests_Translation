579	<clr>Origins and the loss of the homeworld:<clrEnd> If the surviving chronicles are to be believed, the history of the Gaal civilization begins several billion years ago in the globular galaxy of Allir. These chronicles are preserved on the relic comet Fali in the ancient telepathic language used even by the proto-civilizations of the Uglan and the Vaa, and are known as the Fali chronicles. Handed down to the gaalians from the depths of time, they tell not only of the history of the Gaal people but also of what came long before its emergence.<br>The uglans, the first known humanoids (tailed aproids), and the telepathic Vaa people lived in the galaxy of Allir for several million years. Both races were as old as the galaxy itself and possessed vast knowledge of the nature of space and time.<br>While the Vaa were content with the confines of their home galaxy, the uglans were pioneers and actively colonized deep space. Several settlements were also founded in a young spiral galaxy that was then called Ulli-aera. These colonies prospered for a long time, until a catastrophe occurred that forever severed the ties of the uglan colonists with their home galaxy of Allir.<br>Until recently the cause of this catastrophe was unknown. For inexplicable reasons the colonists in the Ulli-aera galaxy lost contact (both telepathic and transport) with the home world of Allir. Deliveries of cargo and information ceased, and the uglan colonists were left to themselves - on planets new to them, in unfamiliar conditions, with a minimum of resources and knowledge. In isolation the great uglan civilization regressed, a process that began with the colonists' loss of many technologies and ended with the forgetting of their roots. Forever cut off from their home, colonial in history but already entirely independent in essence, the colonist society, adapted to the new conditions, began to call itself Gaal. Thus began one of the first inhabitants of the young galaxy of Ulli-aera, a new and distinctive civilization that was destined to grow with its galaxy and see the rise and fall of many peoples.
580	<clr>Early colonial history:<clrEnd> In the beginning the gaalians held a few star systems. Their technology was far below what the uglans originally possessed, and for many long millennia Gaal could not even compare with the technological and informational resources of their ancestors.<br>Early Gaal society was quasi-colonial - possessing several systems that were in essence typical colonies, it developed in such a way as to eliminate the shortcomings of colonialism and assimilate to life on the new planets. Gaal's colonization policy was directed toward the center of the Galaxy, toward the gas clusters, where, according to legend, the uglans' ancient relic planets might be found. The uglan pioneers always created such planets where the climate was unsuitable for transformation and adaptation, and placed on them capsules with messages and navigation instructions for reaching the worlds of Allir. Only by finding the relic planets and their information could the gaalians hope to learn the causes of the catastrophe that cut them off from their world and people. But millennia passed, and no such planets were found, either in the outer arms or in the center of the Galaxy. Gradually the gaalians' historical memory was increasingly displaced by new realities, and knowledge of who the uglans were and why they had come to the Galaxy slowly took on the features of legend until it vanished completely. The very existence of relic planets was declared untrue. The gaalians' appearance, language, and way of life changed; the new civilization lived in new realities and solved new tasks.<br>It was precisely in the search for relic planets that the general line of colonization took shape - toward the center of the Galaxy. The principle of movement was not a hyperjump but linear motion with a multiple slowing of the time function - more art than technology, which the gaalians mastered superbly. In their progressive settlement of star systems Gaal also met the original inhabitants of the Galaxy, who had lived in its central regions for millennia - the liquid-gaseous life form of Nayl, filling the atmospheres and oceans of numerous gas giants; the Oldpra people, dwelling in spores; and later the ancient Beder civilization was discovered, once mighty but having lost its instinct of self-preservation and therefore doomed to constant regression. These were peaceful civilizations that knew no wars; Gaal, itself possessing destructive potential but being a sufficiently enlightened people, entered into constructive dialogue with them. It was a partnership of equals, and coexistence brought its benefits.
581	<clr>The kling invasion:<clrEnd> The period of temporary prosperity lasted until a catastrophe suddenly shook the Galaxy and overturned the entire established world. From the outer reaches came the klings - a hierarchical uncivilized life form whose only purpose of existence was destruction and absorption. Negotiations did not succeed and proved meaningless; the klings methodically seized one planet after another until the Oldpra and Nayl races were completely destroyed. The Beder at first lay far from the klings' advance, and in the aggressor's path stood Gaal, with its rich culture and a mighty war machine that had never yet been used for its intended purpose... A machine that proved utterly useless against so irrationally ruthless an aggressor.<br>Later, studying the remains of the planets seized by the klings, the gaalians would come to stunning conclusions: the klings are not a civilization, not a race, not even a cohabitation of individuals; they are merely a form of existence of a gene structure artificially raised to the level of possessing consciousness. The klings are a phenomenon engendered by distant space, or by the supreme mind itself, solely to test intelligent peoples for their ability to survive. But was it a lesson or a sentence?<br>Gaal was methodically exterminated. Building huge fleets was useless, because the inexplicable paradox was that the klings destroyed large fleets precisely, and with astonishing ease. This dependence was frightening in its absurdity. The Beder people came to help, and were simply crushed but not completely destroyed, thanks to their remoteness.<br>This could have ended in catastrophe for all the surviving inhabitants of the Galaxy, had it not been for completely unexpected support - the Vaa, ancient friends of the uglans, arrived. They told the gaalians the details of ancient events: the klings' invasion of Allir, the resistance and fall of the uglan civilization, the long but effective resistance of the Vaa, and the driving of the klings toward a cluster of galaxies that included the one the uglan pioneers called Ulli-aera. The Vaa followed the klings in order to destroy them once and for all, as they posed the greatest threat to reason itself. But, as it turned out, the complete destruction of the klings was beyond anyone's power, and the Vaa only forced them to leave the galaxy, after which they left themselves. With that, Gaal's contact with the Vaa ended, and the gaalian civilization once again had to rise from the ruins.
582	<clr>Relations with the peoples of the Coalition:<clrEnd> Time passed, and the Galaxy gradually filled with life again. The cyclical laws of the universe led to the appearance of new humanoid forms. Gaal observed, incognito, the development of young civilizations in the outer regions of the Galaxy; when they grew strong and expressed a desire to integrate and build their world together, Gaal had nothing against joining. But ancient wisdom and the experience of millions of years did not let it open all doors at once to those who had only just set out on the thorny path to understanding the universal truths. Only when the situation demanded did the gaalians intervene; usually they stood aside, letting the young races make the ordinary but so necessary mistakes.<br>The relic planet of the uglan pioneers was found; more precisely, it turned out to be a comet with an orbital period of 1500 years - the icy comet Fali. The relic knowledge was read and compared with what Gaal had learned from the Vaa, and the chronicles of the uglans' exodus from Allir and the subsequent death of their home world were restored.<br>The new space-faring peoples know of the gaalian civilization only what the gaalians allow them to know; as yet none of the modern races is able to make proper use of the knowledge that Gaal has accumulated over millions of years. When that time comes and the new humanoids gain maturity and experience, when their minds become ready to receive the ancient knowledge of the uglans, the Vaa, and Gaal, it will happen organically and naturally.<br>Today Gaal maintains friendly relations with the races of the Galactic Coalition; under the Charter, common markets have been established, and educational and tourist complexes have been opened on the planets Riolka and Proon. Detailed information on political structures is not disclosed, although Gaal does have diplomatic missions and holds to positions of peaceful development. They actively buy minerals of various natures and properties, which the gaalians use for molecular operations, mainly to obtain food.
583	<clr>Economic specifics:<clrEnd> A separate problem of the civilization is a high level of drug addiction specific to Gaal. This is the so-called intellectual chemical dependence, in which special narcotic substances are used to enhance mental capacity. Through molecular engineering the gaalians obtain any kind of chemicals from common drugs bought on black markets. Links between gaalian and human drug lords, maloq suppliers, and peleng smugglers are already known. This network is so widespread and powerful that shaking its position in the near future hardly seems possible, although the united galactic special services are working actively in this direction.<br>Gaal's markets, saturated with high-tech and inexpensive products, are enormously popular throughout the Coalition. Where a high level of technology and reliability is needed together with affordability and a low price, gaalian brands are unrivaled. It is Gaal that supplies the markets with cheap, high-quality medical products obtained through molecular synthesis, any technologies, including organic and information ones, and also, illegally, weapons.

585	<clr>Maloq<clrEnd> are a humanoid people who have occupied vast territories of the Galaxy. The maloq race has achieved impressive success in warfare and colonization, while its technological development has been stagnant for a long time. The maloqs are known for their endurance, high reproductive capacity, asceticism, and distrust of intellectual values.<br>Today Maloq plays a leading role in the Coalition's colonization policy, though its strained relations with the Faey race often slow the processes of galactic integration.
586	<clr>Biological features of the species:<clrEnd> The maloqs belong to the class of oxygen-breathing humanoids and are designated orthodal humanoids. This type of being is characterized by physical strength and resistance to external natural factors, weakly active mental activity, and almost absolute omnivorousness. These qualities let the maloqs keep high vitality and fertility even in the most unfavorable habitats.
587	<clr>Origin of civilization and the role of climate:<clrEnd> Maloq civilization arose under the harsh climate of the rocky planet Ramgatru. Lacking sufficient resources, the poorly lit planet did not offer conditions sufficient for prosperity, and for millennia the maloq tribes developed no political or cultural forms. The environment favored only the survival of the species, while development proceeded extremely slowly and often reversed. Archaic nomadic groups of maloqs lived in constant wars, which slowed the progress of their civilization even more.
588	<clr>The cult of strength as the dominant of civilization:<clrEnd> The first rarts devoted themselves entirely to the numerous wars of which the whole early history of the maloq rartans consisted. In essence, the rartan - the traditional form of maloq statehood, echoes of which are still present in their political system - is nothing other than the authority of the strongest warrior over the rest.<br>The entire system of subordination was built on the authority of strength and courage. Strength and military skill are the traditional main positive traits of maloq culture. The weak and infirm were despised, and at the dawn of history there was even a practice of killing the elderly and the disabled.
