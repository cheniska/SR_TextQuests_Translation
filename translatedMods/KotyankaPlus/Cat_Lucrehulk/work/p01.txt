2	<color=248,252,248>An incredible project of joint labor by two races. The faeyans and humans spent a long time developing a ship specialized in deep and thorough search, scanning and analysis of enemy ships - both dominators and the pirate clan - to reveal the enemy's weak points. This ship has a unique system of catalysts and overclocking of the scanning elements' software algorithms, which makes it possible to breach the information defense of practically any ship. However, this system consumes an enormous amount of the ship's energy core resources, and because of this the propulsion system suffers, which reduces travel speed in near-stellar space.</color>
