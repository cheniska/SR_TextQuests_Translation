1	<color=248,252,248>A klissan mothership. Until recently a classified gaalian project, now a formidable war machine. It was found entirely by chance during a routine scan of one of the planets in the Kraa system. There is a suspicion that the gaalians possessed a powerful secret weapon capable of disabling such a giant. The ship's "innards" were removed for further study, and the hull underwent long and painstaking modernization. Once the research was completed, the Makhpella was put up for sale in large numbers - fortunately, there were always connoisseurs of history. The hull is a powerful combination of mobility and durability, able to withstand the heaviest bombardment. Many effects, both positive and negative, on the humanoid and on the ship as a whole were also detected, and it was never possible to record them fully and accurately. Still, that is enough for the Makhpella - its mere appearance makes enemies tremble, and those who catch sight of the guns emerging from its weapon slots start writing their wills. </color>
