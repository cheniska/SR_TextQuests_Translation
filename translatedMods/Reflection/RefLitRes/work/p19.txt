184	Khaynakin Faylwoker was one of those who took a critical view of his own combat capability. No force in the world could make him steer his ship into the thick of military action - the eternal shortage of money had left the poor fellow in the middle of a war with a pitiful laser, a feeble droid and a constantly breaking maloq-made engine. The trophy missile launcher picked up in a recently liberated system (he had heroically saved its container from being shot up by a gaal soldier) was on the verge of breaking, and there was barely enough money to repair the hull and engine, let alone fix the weapons. The only thing Khaynakin liked was his fuel tank, and only because it was made by conscientious gaals and didn't break after every hyperjump. Yes, Faylwoker had had money once. Now it was time to contemplate robbing trade liners - and even then, only contemplate. The industrial laser could scare off an asteroid at best.<br>Probably his ranger career inspired no one - otherwise why would he be summoned to the Rangers' Center with such an official request? Probably the Galactic Council was not at all pleased to find Faylwoker in the Prokrut trade center at the very height of the battle for Antares. It was also upsetting that you can't earn much from minerals off shot-down asteroids - and it would have been nice to build up at least some fortune before his photo was hung on the wall of shame and he was driven out by the scruff from all the nearby planets.
185	- I've never seen anything like it on other planets, - Khaynakin admitted, looking around - in the distance the domes of churches could be seen, seemingly no less ancient than the government building itself.<br>- They're almost everywhere... except on maloq planets, where they're very rare - you know, they don't welcome cultural values there, - Palladi explained. - It's just that over the past centuries cities improved, governments moved into newer buildings, and the fountains remained in the historic parts of the city. Khalgalla is one of the few planets where almost nothing has changed, we value our heritage. This building, for example, is three hundred and five years old, we moved here from the capital after the Klissan War began. The roof and upper floors, though, were destroyed anyway during the attack of their fleet on the system, but those were stray volleys, they didn't shoot at us deliberately. Unfortunately, the architect and the entire crew of builders died along with a third of the planet's population, so we had to patch up the building ourselves, and in place of the destroyed part we overlaid a hologram based on the early blueprints. Oh, I've been talking too much. Come on, they're waiting for you.<br>Palladi gestured for Faylwoker to follow and was the first to enter the gate. Khaynakin followed, turning his head in all directions, trying not to miss anything, since it was his first time on Khalgalla, and the planet made a huge impression on him - you don't see such an organic combination of historic and modern architecture everywhere. Even on his native Earth, where cultural heritage was also, on the whole, valued, the government sat in the most guarded and ultramodern sector of the planet, preferring to demonstrate its power rather than hide it.

Palladi led the ranger to a small room off the main corridors and told him to wait while he announced the guest's arrival.
