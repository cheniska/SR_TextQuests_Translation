21	POSTSCRIPT
22	Good day, Ranger Sanderson's diary, and also rangers and my colleagues who will ever decide to listen to these recordings! You have already heard from Melani how we met, but since for me too this event was quite momentous, I decided to tell about it as well, so here is my crazy story.<br>Allow me to introduce myself: lyakusha Rakhoyta. I became a pirate long ago, mostly controlling my home system Pkhedoc and neighboring Altair.<br>(- We're not writing your biography here, my diary isn't made of rubber, you know! You'd start from childhood next! - Melani snorted, sitting nearby.<br>- Want to hear it? - Rakhoyta smirked. - I'll tell you later.<br>- Pff! I'm not interested! Get to the point!<br>- Baby, you started your story with childhood yourself, so let me tell it the way I want.<br>- ...Just talk already!)
23	I won't dwell on this in detail: some people are impatient here. Besides, you all know what pirates usually do: robbed here, shot up there.<br>One wonderfully terrible day I was collecting minerals on the outskirts of the system, and suddenly my radar picked up a human ship that had arrived in Pkhedoc. I loaded the last mineral aboard and flew to meet it. The scanner had already reported everything about this vessel and its pilot. I was going to rob... that is, demand payment for intruding on my territory from a young human girl in an old tub with two obviously secondhand guns and nonexistent defense. I was undoubtedly lucky: the girl had surely barely received her ranger badge.<br>- Well... Let's see what kind of fruit this Melani Sanderson is...<br>I rubbed my hands, anticipating easy prey, and contacted the ship.<br>- Hello, ranger! Where are we headed? - I inquired.<br>From the display a tanned human girl with short blond hair smiled at me uncertainly. Rather short in stature. Her features were quite typical for a human, but rather pleasant and regular, except that her nose was slightly snub. Her bright blue eyes, nevertheless, looked interested rather than frightened.<br>- To the planet Shukheo - I have an assignment, - she answered.
24	- An assignment, then? - I narrowed my eyes, trying to guess how long ago she had finished that Academy of theirs. - The first, right?<br>- Well, almost... - the girl laughed timidly. - The second.<br>I smirked. Almost guessed it. A fledgling had landed right in my cunning paws. What an airhead: just graduated and immediately raced off to a peleng system. What was I to do with her? Strangely enough, not one of my hands rose against her. Just look at those enraptured-and-frightened big blue eyes! What was wrong with me? I didn't want to take her last money at all. Very little in her account. I'd heard that novice rangers have a hard time: assignments pay little as it is, and on top of that our kind doesn't miss a single one. I wiggled my gills.<br>- Oh, all right, fly on, little one!<br>What are you doing, idiot? She'll spread it all over the galaxy that lyakusha Rakhoyta is losing his grip, letting snot-nosed girls go in all directions. But this delight and happiness in her beautiful blue eyes... No one had looked at me like that in a long time. Yes, perhaps never.<br>- But next time have the toll ready. I'll remember you!
25	- Of course! Thank you! - this girl smiled even wider.<br>What is this! What are they doing to them at the Academy now? The girl smiled so infectiously and sweetly that I too felt somehow cheerful and light of heart. Why was she so happy? I'm a pirate, aren't I?<br>- Are you so happy to share what you've earned with others? - I asked, unable to hold back.<br>- No, I've just never seen a real pirate!<br>What a darling! Who let her into the rangers at all?<br>- I'm not just a pirate, baby - I'm lyakusha Rakhoyta! Remember that name!<br>I cut the connection, but her face still stood before my eyes. Suddenly another ranger ship came out of hyperspace next to us and immediately opened fire on me. With its very first shot that scoundrel damaged my best gun, and my defense weakened noticeably.<br>- Why, you... bald monkey, now you'll get it from me!.. - I hissed viciously and fired all weapons.<br>Suddenly a shot rang out from the side, and the ranger's ship jerked awkwardly. I looked at the onboard computer screen in bewilderment. I had been firing, of course, but not from that side! A gun simply couldn't have fired into his rear without breaking a heap of laws of physics. So who fired, Makhpella take me?! The enemy ship was already hastily withdrawing, and on the screen the smiling face framed by light brown hair appeared again.
26	- Afakay, please bring the message.<br>- Yes, of course, - he got up and disappeared for a while.<br>I sat sulking and pretending the peleng across from me did not exist. Although from time to time I allowed myself to throw withering glances in his direction. The culprit of my spoiled mood retained his former composure. At last Afakay returned, holding a small box in his hands.<br>- What is that? - asked the maloq.<br>- A latest-model recording device from my planet, - Afakay replied, switching something.<br>- And what's in it?<br>- Information about our assignment.<br>- Is it encrypted? - I asked, giving up on being offended and moving closer.<br>The faeyan turned to me for a moment, and then started clicking the device again.<br>- No, I was just given a password and told to unlock it when we all gather on the planet.<br>The pause was broken by the peleng's chuckle.<br>- Is it that simple? You faeyans have finally realized that your precautions are useless anyway and decided to abandon them?<br>- No, - Afakay answered with slight irritation. - The box was with me the whole time, there was no need for additional precautions!
27	- Well, now I think we're even? - the girl's blue eyes flashed boldly, like the brightest stars of the galaxy.<br>Still in some bewilderment, I mentally agreed with her. But, not wanting to show that her help had really come in handy for me, the Scourge of Pkhedoc, I replied with a dismissive wave of my hand:<br>- I would have dealt with him myself! But fine! The debt is canceled. For now... See you soon!<br>The ship of the young defender of pirates was already flying away, and I was still thinking about the strange ranger. Why did she act that way? Who knows... Deciding that this was one more of the Universe's unsolvable mysteries, I turned around and flew off. Suddenly a suspicious red dot flickered on the radar.<br>- What is it now?<br>I requested data from the computer and winced. Just what I needed right now! Sighing, I contacted the familiar vessel.<br>- Hey, hello, pal! - the maloq guffawed happily. - I've marked easy prey here! Want to join in?<br>I snorted.<br>- Not a ranger, by any chance?
28	- Yeah, yeah, no defense, practically no guns to speak of. So how about it? I'm kind today, I'll even share something.<br>- You're celebrating too soon, - I snorted.<br>- What? - Ramgert didn't understand.<br>- The thing is, I was already chasing this little boat, I just got a bit distracted by minerals, - I lied confidently, feverishly figuring out how to pull the funny girl with blue eyes out of this mess.<br>- So that's ho-o-ow... - the maloq drawled distrustfully.<br>- Exactly! - I nodded and began catching up with Sanderson's ship. - But since you so nobly decided to share the meager loot with me, I'll do you the same honor, Ramgert. Have you checked the account yet?<br>- Nah, haven't had time yet.<br>- I'll look... Aha, we can shake a couple of thousand out of her, so a grand each, deal? - I asked calmly, while my mouth went dry with excitement.
29	If the bambay had refused, honest to goodness, I would have spat on everything and smashed his ship, whatever it cost me, but fortunately Ramgert didn't notice the trick and agreed. As I approached the ranger ship, I realized that this dim girl hadn't noticed anything, sighed heavily and took aim. A precise hit knocked out her engine, and a second one punctured the tank.<br>- Sorry, little one, - I muttered regretfully and transferred a thousand credits to Ramgert's account. - But your debt seems to have grown...<br>The maloq pirate flew off, quite pleased with himself, and I also jumped to Altair, leaving Melani Sanderson in a disabled ship on the edge of the system. I was sure her fellow rangers would pick her up soon. And I was also absolutely sure that she would have a chance to repay me the debt, because I had just realized that I'd fallen head over gills for a blue-eyed human girl who likes pirates. And although this had happened to me for the first time, I knew she wouldn't get rid of me so easily now. Or I'm not lyakusha Rakhoyta!
30	(- Melani? Did something get in your eye?<br>- ...Yes.<br>- Sure? Or are you crying after all?<br>- Shut up, you tailed romantic... - Melani muttered and sniffled once more, touched.<br>The pirate closed his eyes contentedly and switched off the recording device.)
