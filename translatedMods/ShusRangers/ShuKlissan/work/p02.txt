41	Years went into studying the reasons for the klissans' return and, no less important - the nature of their modern semi-mechanical metamorphoses. But only to understand that, once again, the pirates were behind it all. Taught by the bitter experience of Rachekhan, who led to the Klissan War, the corsairs went much further than their predecessor, making use of the works of the renegade dalani Alakay, who went on the run because of his scientific work on nanites capable of seizing control of the klissan life form.<br>    As a result of your swift attack on Alakay, unprepared for an instant response, the Handler facility, from which manipulations of the nanite-infected Makhpella were carried out, was destroyed along with her. At the last moment the Handler's team itself activated the station's self-destruct system, unable to bear the burden imposed on them - as a result nothing was left of Alakay's research except scattered atomic dust. Nothing could survive the simultaneous detonation of ten quark bombs planted as a safeguard against unforeseen circumstances.<br>    You, however, retired with dignity, enjoying a peaceful life and watching proudly in Galanet reports how your colleagues dealt with one dominator brain center after another. Too bad that peace did not last long - soon after the united Coalition forces staged the Second Gralgar, driving the remnants of the dominators there, huge deposits of dizodan were discovered on the planet where your mansion stood, which gave the whole system sudden strategic importance. The dominators' desperate counterattack was not long in coming, and soon you found yourself in the midst of seized territories. Unable to watch the iron invaders burn the megacities standing over the deposits, the Ranger in you awoke again, and you joined the local resistance, soon dying heroically defending a group of civilians from dominator combat robots that had decided to set up yet another factory on the site of your shelter. Since then the name ranger will remain forever in the ages, carved on the Heroes' Memorial of the planet that, if only briefly, became your new home.
42	After the elimination of both the klissan and dominator threats you received your share of glory. However, the appearance of your face on all the screens of the galaxy led, a few days later, to a well-organized group of pirate liquidators attacking your ship, and you survived by a miracle. Realizing that the pirates would never leave alone the one who had uncovered their insidious plans and decapitated their organization, you decided simply to disappear. The Coalition's special services staged the death of the great hero in a lone battle against an enemy outnumbering him several times over, and then arranged a lavish funeral for the hero of the Coalition, the live broadcast of which you watched on stereovision.<br> <br>    New documents and a new name, plus a large bank account, gave you the freedom to choose your future fate. Now you could rest at the best interstellar resorts until the end of time. Or, alternatively, accept the offer of the Coalition intelligence chief and become a mentor of a new generation of special agents. Or you could take up writing memoirs in the style of a science fiction novel, or learn to play an ancient human instrument - the accordion...<br> <br>    But one day, happening to be near the building of a special military training center, you saw a poster: "The Galaxy needs Space Rangers!", which helped you make the final decision...
43	To say that you made a weighty contribution to ending the war in the Coalition's favor is to say nothing. Having personally influenced all the key events that led to the complete and categorical collapse of both the dominators and the pirates, you almost instantly became everyone's darling as soon as most of the documents no longer requiring secrecy reached the public press. For the next few years your face constantly flashed on screens and in newspapers, and a couple of books were even written about your Ranger career. Besides that, you were constantly invited to be either a showman or a politician. It struck you at once, though, that it was one and the same profession, only called by different names for some reason. So you politely but firmly declined all offers of that kind. <br> <br>    When peace in the inhabited arm of the galaxy finally grew strong and nothing threatened it anymore, the Ranger corps was disbanded with honors, with a lavish ceremony held for the occasion and awards for all participants in the fighting. You were invited as the host of the celebration, and were very glad to see old comrades, with many of whom you had fought shoulder to shoulder. <br> <br>    Most of the former Rangers returned to peaceful life, and some, especially the hot-blooded lads, you offered to work together again and founded an elite security agency. Within a couple of years the whole galaxy knew of your firm and the quality of its services, after which you were literally buried in orders. No pirates or extremists could match your employees, former Coalition fighters, in skill. Having quickly secured a comfortable existence, you nevertheless headed the firm into deep old age, and retired as a highly respected person.

47	After long deliberation you agreed to head the pirate community. For this, however, you had to leave the ranks of the Space Rangers and give up all the benefits and privileges due to every veteran who bravely defended the Coalition's interests.<br> <br>    In return you gained the chance to bring back the times of former pirate romanticism, which you successfully realized. Thanks to you, the word of honor of a noble pirate regained its former value!<br> <br>    Despite your ambiguous reputation, you lived a long and happy life, enjoying enormous respect not only among your colleagues in the brotherhood but also among those who fought them. And the legends of your courage and justice were passed from generation to generation not only with the newest technical devices but also as in the good old days: from human to maloq, and from maloq via a peleng to everyone else - naturally, for a separate fee. Except that this did not apply to the dominators, who generally accepted no fee at all and were in no hurry to pay themselves. This, however, did not save them from defeat - using the Coalition's military shield, your people managed to penetrate the dominators' rear and liquidate all the remaining brain centers, proving once and for all that pirates under your leadership are not merely a gang deserving respect.
48	You put your faithful ship into permanent parking and took on important affairs together with other pirate activists and nominees. Naturally, your authority was untouchable, and one vote of yours equaled three ordinary ones. Other pirates respected you for the knowledge acquired back when you were a Ranger. Hard to believe, but some pirate atamans, and even khans, had very vague notions of such sciences as mathematics or even astronomy. You soon tired of this and introduced mandatory courses to raise the pirates' educational level, taught under the leadership of the best faeyan specialists... Well, at least you tried. All your efforts were wiped out by the sudden activation of the surviving dominator brain centers - having built several dozen shipyards, whose number, as had earlier been supposed, was meant to be limited by the imperfection of the software, the dominators literally swept away the frontier of the controlled worlds - pirate ships did not even have time to retreat. In the end, deprived of the barrier of Coalition warships that had previously taken all the damage, and of one of its best fighters, the Pirate Clan lost this war to the soulless machines, and half a century later any other sapient life in the whole Galaxy was completely exterminated, names were replaced by identification numbers, and there was no place among them for the heroes of the past.
