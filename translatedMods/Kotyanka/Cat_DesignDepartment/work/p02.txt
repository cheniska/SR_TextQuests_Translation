65	Besides the obvious, some event parameters contain lists of ships or items. First of all, you need to understand that all the options separated by commas in such lists mean a choice among options of the OR type, that is, EITHER this, OR that, OR the other. If the separators are && signs instead of commas, it means AND. That is, both this AND that, AND the other.
66	Next, the breakdown of randomization should be described. For example, we have a string like Blueprint_Hull_Own_1, which is split by '_' separators into four parts. The first is the key item type. The second is the specific list from which the item will be taken (both items and blueprints from the mod are created from common lists). In this case it will be a hull blueprint from the ship of this event, since the third segment is marked 'Own'. Own must not be put in items for event types that have no ships. Rnd could also be put in place of Own, which would mean choosing a random hull blueprint from the mod list, or a number denoting the specific number of the hull blueprint in the mod list. The last, after a separator, is a number denoting the quantity of these items, in this case 1. Instead of this number you could also set a range 1:5, which would mean a spread of one to five items of this type (it is important to remember that the quantity indicator must ALWAYS come last in an item string). The value '_Equipment_Rnd_' or '_Equipment_Own_' should be read as random equipment from all equipment lists, or random equipment from the board of the event ship, respectively. Instead of 'Equipment' you can also specify the name of a specific section, such as 'Engine', 'FuelTank' and so on.
67	In some cases the second and third segments of a type like 'Hull_Own' may be absent altogether, for example NirgendAzur_1:5. This means that a specific type cannot be specified for these kinds of items. For the FakeBlueprint type, for example, a third segment cannot be specified, since for a fake blueprint the specific item number does not matter anyway.
68	With lists of ships the situation is similar, but specifying the ship type is pointless here, since there is only one list of templates for them. Instead, the second segment can simply be Rnd or a specific template number.
